## Contributed notes (version 1.0.2)

- fNIRS recordings of 11 participants during electrical pain stimulation (VAS 3/7 for electrical stimulation only) and thermal pain stimulation, finger tapping and motor imagery (Yücel et al. 2015, Scientific Reports 5:9469, doi:10.1038/srep09469; Yücel et al. 2015, Neurophotonics 2(3):035005, doi:10.1117/1.NPh.2.3.035005).
- Time unit: the SNIRF files of all 46 recordings declare the time unit "unknown". Read as seconds, their time axes give a sampling rate of 50 Hz, equal to SamplingFrequency in every nirs.json; nothing points to milliseconds.
